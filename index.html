The sound lab

SIREN picks every track's sounds from its sound bank: for each style, a short list of sounds per layer (bass, arp, hook, pad, stabs, FX). Picking that bank is the biggest job left before SIREN ships on the FM-1, and your ears can help.

  1. Pick a style below.
  2. Listen to each sound (▶), remove what doesn't fit (×), add what's missing (+ add a sound).
  3. Try it: go to Play and press ▶. SIREN now picks from your bank (‹ › on a layer goes through its sounds).
  4. Send it to me with the button on the right. Your bank is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.

SIREN balances the sounds of its own bank automatically. A sound you add plays at its raw level for now: judge the sound, not how loud it is.

Send your bank

Sends your bank, your name and your notes straight to sodwavecontact@gmail.com. No email app, no attachment. You can send again after more changes.

    How to play
    
    
      
      
    1. ▶ Play. SIREN composes an endless rave: the track, its sections (intro, build, drop, break…), its sounds. Pick a style at the bottom.
      2. 
      
    2. The composition (the big pads): ask for a DROP, a BUILD, a BREAK, a FILL, a new melody, a key change, the next track… A pad stays lit until it happens, on the beat.
      3. 
      
    3. The DJ: FILTER (left goes muffled, right goes thin), INTENSITY, SPACE (reverb and echo), MELODY. Drag a knob up or down.
      4. 
      
    4. The mixer: every layer has its volume, filter, space, ‹ › to change its sound, ↻ for another motif, M to mute. Click its name to take it in hand: it always plays.
      5. 
      
    5. DJ effects: hold an effect down (roll, filter, echo, cut…); let go and the music is back where it was.
      6. 
    
    
    
    SIREN keeps the mix clean on its own: at most 3 melodic layers at once (the ones waiting their turn say "waiting"), every sound at the right level.
    
    
    Want to help? The Sound lab tab lets you build your own sound bank and send it to me.
    
    
    Keyboard: space = play, F = show mode. Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
